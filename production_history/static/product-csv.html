Import ShopVox products

ProductsWorkspace

1. Choose your product CSV

Preview the export, review the products, and select the items to add. Existing catalogue records keep their saved values.

Up to 1,000 source rows. This importer adds active products with a positive basic unit price. Grid prices, formulas and zero-price rows need a separate pricing review. Images and inventory quantities are not included.

2. Review your products

The CSV does not identify a currency or VAT rate. Confirm that selected prices are in BSD before VAT, and enter a VAT rate for each selected product. ShopVox's Taxable flag is shown for reference.
