My time clock

Standard shift: Monday–Friday, 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. Sign-in does not clock you in. Overtime starts after eight counted hours and is recorded by the minute. All time worked on Saturday and Sunday is eligible for time in lieu. Each eligible minute earns one minute off after manager approval.

On weekdays, time before 9:00 a.m. counts only after an approved early start. Weekend clock-ins count from the actual clock-in time. No lunch deduction is automatic; time while clocked out is excluded.

Requesting time off

Submit planned time-off requests in Business Manager under HR → Time Off. Personal time off must be requested at least 24 hours before it starts. Vacation must be requested at least 14 calendar days before the first day of vacation. Planned requests that don’t meet these deadlines won’t be approved. Submitting a request does not mean it has been approved.

If you’re sick or have an emergency, notify your manager as soon as you can. These deadlines do not override leave entitlements under the law.

Requests are reviewed by an HR manager. Vacation and other leave balances are not calculated here.

For part of a day, use the same start and end date and describe the hours in your note. A full working day is 8 hours.

Time in lieu balance

Approved time-off requests deduct from this balance; cancelling approved lieu leave restores it. Pending requests do not reserve time.
